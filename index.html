FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly is the WhatsApp AI assistant?

It's a 24×7 AI agent that runs on your own WhatsApp Business number. It answers product enquiries from your catalogue, sends spec-sheet PDFs, qualifies and scores leads, cross-sells spare parts, and alerts your team the moment a hot lead appears — all managed from a single admin CRM dashboard.

Do I need my own WhatsApp Business number?

Yes — you provide a verified Meta WhatsApp Business number and we connect the AI to it. Cloud hosting, servers and storage are fully managed by Elevate Automation, so there's nothing for you to set up on the tech side.

How does the AI know my products?

You share your product PDFs and photos, and we ingest them through an AI ETL pipeline. The assistant then answers buyer questions accurately from your catalogue and delivers the exact spec sheet inside the chat — no wrong information.

How long until we're live?

Typically 2–3 weeks from kick-off and catalogue handover. Most clients are live and taking automated enquiries in under three weeks.

What does it cost?

Pricing is tailored to your catalogue size, monthly conversation volume and any integrations you need. We offer Starter, Growth and Enterprise plans — contact us for a custom quote and we'll recommend the right fit.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Hosting and storage are fully managed by us, with dedicated servers and daily backups available on Enterprise. Your catalogue and buyer data remain yours.
